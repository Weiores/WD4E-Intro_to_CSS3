About Singapore

Why Singapore?

Singapore is a small but incredibly diverse city-state that offers a unique blend of cultures, cuisines, and natural wonders. From its bustling street markets to its pristine nature reserves, such as Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve, Singapore is a haven for birdwatchers and nature enthusiasts alike. The city’s commitment to green spaces and biodiversity has created a thriving habitat for over 400 bird species, making it a must-visit destination for anyone interested in avian life.

Singapore's tropical climate means it's warm and humid throughout the year, so pack light, breathable clothing and rain gear.

The city is also very walkable and has an excellent public transportation system, making it easy to explore its many parks and nature reserves where you can spot a wide variety of birds, from colorful kingfishers to majestic hornbills. Don't forget to bring your binoculars and a good field guide to help you identify the incredible variety of birds you'll encounter. Whether you're exploring the lush greenery of the Botanic Gardens or the coastal mangroves, Singapore offers an unforgettable birdwatching experience that combines urban convenience with natural beauty.

Top Travel Tips

  • Pack Light

    Singapore is a small city, so you won't need much luggage.

  • English is the Lingua Franca

    English is widely spoken and understood, making communication easy.

  • Prepare Cash!

    While credit cards are widely accepted, having some cash on hand is always a good idea.

  • Ask the locals

    Locals can provide valuable insights and recommendations for hidden gems and authentic experiences.
